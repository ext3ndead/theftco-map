Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Theft & Co ("we") publishes our own short videos about GTA 6 to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts using an internal tool. The tool is used only by the account owner.

What we access

When the account owner connects a TikTok or Instagram account, we receive an access token from that platform. We use it only to upload and publish our own videos to that same account. We do not access, collect or store data about other users.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok or Meta account settings.

Telegram bot and map

Our Telegram bot and GTA 6 map store only what is needed to work: your Telegram user id when you start the bot, and places you save on the map (kept in Telegram's own cloud storage).

Questions: write to our Telegram bot @theftncobot.
